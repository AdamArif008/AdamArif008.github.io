About Me

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Physics and Astronomy at Binghamton University, SUNY. My research focuses on 2D magnetic materials, an exciting area in modern physics.

I am originally from Meherpur, Bangladesh. I am passionate about using my knowledge and position to contribute to both science and society.

Outside of research, I enjoy photography—especially nature photography. I like capturing the beauty of landscapes and natural environments.

Research

My work focuses on 2D magnetic materials and their potential applications in future electronic and spintronic technologies.
