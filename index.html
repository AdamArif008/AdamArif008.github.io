About Me

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Physics and Astronomy at Binghamton University, SUNY. My research focuses on 2D magnetic materials, an exciting area in modern physics with potential applications in future electronic and spintronic technologies.

I am originally from Meherpur, Bangladesh. I am passionate about using my knowledge and position to contribute meaningfully to both science and society.

Outside of research, I enjoy photography, especially nature photography. I like capturing landscapes and natural environments that reflect the beauty of the world around us.

Research

My work explores the properties and potential applications of two-dimensional magnetic materials. I am interested in understanding how these materials can contribute to future advances in physics, electronics, and spintronics.

Social Commitment

I am deeply committed to helping poor and elderly people in Meherpur, Bangladesh who struggle to afford basic necessities such as food, clothing, and medicine.

I also want to support education for underprivileged children, because I believe education can create dignity, opportunity, and long-term positive change.
