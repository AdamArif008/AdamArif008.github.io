About Me

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Physics and Astronomy at Binghamton University, SUNY. My academic work is centered on understanding the properties and potential of advanced materials, with a particular focus on 2D magnetic materials.

I am originally from Meherpur, Bangladesh. Alongside my research, I care deeply about social responsibility and using my education and position to contribute meaningfully to society.

Research

My research focuses on 2D magnetic materials, an exciting area in modern physics with potential applications in future electronic and spintronic technologies. I am interested in exploring the fundamental behavior of these materials and their relevance for next-generation scientific and technological development.

Social Commitment

I am deeply interested in social work, especially for poor and elderly people in Meherpur, Bangladesh. I know that many people there cannot afford basic necessities such as clothes, food, and medicine.

My goal is to help underprivileged and elderly individuals live with greater dignity, care, and support during the later stages of their lives.

I also want to support poor children in their education. I believe that access to education can change lives, and I hope to contribute to a future where children from disadvantaged backgrounds have better opportunities to learn, grow, and succeed.
