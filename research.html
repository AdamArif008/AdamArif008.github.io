Research

My academic work focuses on 2D magnetic materials, an exciting and rapidly growing area in condensed matter physics.

Research Overview & Methodology

My research begins with the synthesis of two-dimensional magnetic materials using the Chemical Vapor Transport (CVT) method at Zhong Lin Lab, Binghamton University, SUNY.

Below are optical microscope images of layered crystals synthesized in our research group, where the rectangular grid corresponds to a millimeter (mm) scale.

Optical images of layered single crystals synthesized in our research group, including CrPS₄, NbFeTe₂, Fe₃GeTe₂, MnPS₃, MnPSe₃, WSe₂, FePS₃, FePSe₃, and SnSe₂.

Following synthesis, we perform structural and compositional characterization at the Analytical and Diagnostics Laboratory (ADL), including X-ray Diffraction (XRD), Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM), Energy Dispersive X-ray Spectroscopy (EDS), and Atomic Force Microscopy (AFM).

We further investigate optical and magnetic properties using Linear Dichroism (LD), Raman spectroscopy, and Reflective Magneto-Circular Dichroism (RMCD) measurements at Zhong Lin Lab.
